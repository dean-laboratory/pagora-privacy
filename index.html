Pagora Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Pagora application for Android (the "Application"), developed by pqzmggg (the "Developer"), handles information in connection with its use. The Application organizes the PDF and ZIP (CBZ) books stored in the user's Google Drive into a library and displays them for reading.

1. Information Collected by the Developer

The Developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any personal information of users. The Developer operates no servers for the Application, and the Application requires no account other than the user's own Google account. All information described in this Policy is processed on the user's device or exchanged directly between the device and Google. The Application contains no advertising, analytics, or crash reporting components.

2. Google Account and Google Drive Data

When the user chooses to connect Google Drive, the Application requests read-only access to the user's Google Drive (the drive.readonly scope) through Google's authorization service. The Application does not receive or store the user's Google password.

With this access, the Application uses the following Google Drive data solely to provide its features to the user:

  1. The names, folder locations, sizes, modification times, checksums, and thumbnail images of PDF and ZIP (CBZ) files, and the names and structure of folders, in order to find books, build the library, and sort books into categories automatically on the device.
  2. The contents of the files that the user chooses to download, in order to keep them on the device and display their pages. When the user has read most of a volume in a series, the Application may download the next volume of the same series in advance.
  3. Parts of ZIP (CBZ) files, in order to create cover images.

The Application only reads Google Drive data. It does not create, modify, move, or delete any file in the user's Google Drive. Google Drive data is requested by the device directly from Google and is not transmitted to the Developer or to any other party.

The Application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Drive data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not read by any person.

3. Permissions and Their Purposes

  1. Internet access: to obtain authorization from Google and to read the list and contents of files in the user's Google Drive.

4. Information Stored on the Device and Its Deletion

For its operation, the Application retains the following information solely in the Application's private storage on the user's device:

  1. The library: the file information described in Section 2(1), the categories assigned to each book, reading positions and progress, and bookmarks.
  2. Books that the user has downloaded, so that they can be read on the device, and cover images, kept as a cache to display the library quickly.
  3. Reader settings and whether Google Drive has been connected.

Application backup is disabled, and this information is therefore not copied to any cloud backup. The user may delete a downloaded book at any time by long-pressing it in the library, and may delete all such information at any time by clearing the Application's storage or by uninstalling the Application. Books that are no longer found in Google Drive are removed from the library 30 days after they disappear.

5. Revoking Access

The user may disconnect Google Drive within the Application at any time. The user may also revoke the Application's access to the Google account at any time on the Google Account third-party access page. After access is revoked, the Application can no longer read Google Drive data.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

The Developer does not provide, sell, or transfer any information to third parties. Requests for authorization and for Google Drive data are sent only to Google and are handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The Application is not directed at children, and the Developer does not collect information from any person, including children.

8. Changes to This Policy

Any amendment to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page together with its effective date.

9. Contact

Inquiries concerning this Privacy Policy may be directed to pqzmggg@gmail.com.
